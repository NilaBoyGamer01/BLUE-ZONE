where it's going

Our story

BLUE-ZONE began in 2025 as a small survival server run by a handful of friends who wanted somewhere to build without the noise of a giant public network. The BlueX Team formed to keep it running, and the world grew one invite at a time.

Today it's a full community: a survival world, an event calendar, a gallery of member builds, and a Discord that never really sleeps.

What we're building toward

  • A survival world that stays fair, griefer-free, and worth logging back into.
  • Regular events that anyone can join solo.
  • A gallery that actually celebrates member work.
  • A BlueX Team that stays reachable, not a wall of untouchable admins.

Server Rules

  1. Be respectful — no harassment, hate speech, or targeted drama.
  2. No griefing, stealing, or claim-breaking on the survival world.
  3. Keep NSFW, spam and self-promo out of every channel.
  4. Post builds and screenshots in #showcase, not #general.
  5. Follow BlueX Team and moderator instructions during events.
